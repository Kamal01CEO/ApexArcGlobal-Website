## Who Is Kamal Rai?

Kamal Rai is a student at LPU (Lovely Professional University) and the founder of **ApexArcGlobal**, an AI infrastructure company that builds AI employees for businesses.

He started building AI systems because he couldn't afford a team. Now he helps other founders and companies do the same — automate operations without hiring.

## What ApexArcGlobal Does

ApexArcGlobal builds:

- **AI employees** that handle lead generation, outreach, and client communication
- **Persistent memory systems** so AI remembers across sessions
- **Voice interfaces** for hands-free operation
- **Production infrastructure** — not demos, not pilots, but systems that actually work

## The Origin Story

I was sitting in my hostel at LPU with zero money, zero team, and a problem: I needed to run a business but couldn't hire anyone.

So I built AI employees instead.

They don't sleep. They don't quit. They find prospects, draft outreach, handle follow-ups, and flag me when action is needed.

The first month, they found 200 prospects and sent 150 emails. I got 23 replies. 8 calls. 2 clients.

That proved the concept: one person with the right AI infrastructure can operate like a five-person team.
